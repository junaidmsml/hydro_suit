Isohyetal Method for Hilly & Varied Terrain

Draw rainfall contours across your basin and calculate area-weighted average using the Isohyetal Method — ideal for mountainous and uneven terrain.

What This Module Does

Contour Map Generation

Draws rainfall isohyets (contour lines) across your basin using IDW interpolation.

Isohyetal Average

Calculates basin average using the area between contour lines — more accurate than Thiessen for hilly areas.

Station Plotting

Plots each station on the map with its rainfall value labeled.

Method Comparison

Compare Isohyetal results with Arithmetic Mean to understand terrain effects.
